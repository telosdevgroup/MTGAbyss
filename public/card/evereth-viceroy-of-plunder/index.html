In the heart of the Abyss, Evereth, Viceroy of Plunder, soars on obsidian wings. His eyes are twin embers, burning with hunger for the treasures he commands his minions to plunder. With a sacrifice offered at his altar, Evereth's power grows, and if it's treasure you've given, his thirst for blood is quenched until end of turn. But beware, when the viceroy falls, he leaves behind a fiery wake, dealing damage equal to his power to each opponent.
