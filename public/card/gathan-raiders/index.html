In the heart of the Abyss, where light is but a rumor, the Gathan Raiders emerge from their sunken city, hunger carved into their granite features. Their eyes glow like embers, burning with the fury of a people starved for conquest. Clad in rusted mail and wielding serrated blades forged from the bones of ancient leviathans, they wade through the ink-black waters, their voices raised in a battle cry that sends shivers down the spine of even the bravest adventurer. They come not as allies, but as harbingers of chaos, their hellish power growing with each card consigned to the abyss of your hand. Will you join them in their relentless crusade, or be swallowed by their tidal wave of wrath?
