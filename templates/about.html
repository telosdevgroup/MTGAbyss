Behind the Project

About AvaScry

Neural discovery, visual galleries, and intelligent Commander deck synergy for Magic: The Gathering players, collectors, and deckbuilders.

Why AvaScry Exists

Traditional card search engines excel at rigid text lookups: syntax filters for exact mana costs, rules keywords, or collector numbers. But building real Magic decks requires understanding synergy, flavor, gameplay texture, and artistic lineage—qualities that rigid database schemas cannot easily capture.

AvaScry was created to bridge this gap. Rather than serving as a plain database duplicate, AvaScry blends deep semantic analysis with modern web performance to help players discover cards they would otherwise never have found.

Core Features & Capabilities

🧠

Neural Similar Engine

Powered by 4,096-dimensional high-parameter embeddings that parse rules mechanics, card role, tempo, and card-type interactions to recommend contextually resonant alternatives.

⚡

SmartDeck Builder

An automated Commander archetype synthesizer that evaluates curve, mana fixing, draw engines, interaction packages, and win-conditions tailored directly to your commander's strategy.

🎨

Live Art Gallery

A cinematic, full-bleed artwork experience celebrating the illustrators behind three decades of fantasy art, with comprehensive artist catalogs and curated set spotlights.

🤖

AI & Open Machine Access

First-class content negotiation providing pristine Markdown (.md) and JSON (.json) representations for LLMs, autonomous agents, and third-party tools.
