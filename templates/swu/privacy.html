Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

AvaScry SWU operates as a free, open reference catalog. We do not require accounts or user registration to access card scans, stats, or rules data. When you submit feedback via our Contact form, we collect your provided name, contact email or Discord handle, and message details solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

We use standard session cookies and browser local storage strictly to remember user display preferences (such as Scans view vs. Text List view). We do not sell or monetize personal data.

3. Analytics & Infrastructure

We collect anonymous server logs (HTTP status, user-agent, requested URL, response time) for server health, DDoS mitigation, and search index optimization.

4. Third-Party Services

External card images are hosted and delivered via verified official Content Delivery Networks. Third-party fonts are loaded securely via Google Fonts.

5. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, please contact our team via the Contact Form.
