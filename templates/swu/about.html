About AvaScry SWU

Canonical cards, aspect interactions, and rules engine for Star Wars: Unlimited.

Mission & Architecture

AvaScry SWU is a dedicated, ultra-fast card database and rules search engine for Star Wars: Unlimited, the trading card game designed by Fantasy Flight Games and Lucasfilm Ltd.

Built on the AvaScry high-performance network infrastructure, our goal is to give players, tournament judges, and deck builders instant access to complete card scans, leader flip states, aspect synergies, and arena classifications with zero bloat.

Data Verification & Coverage

All card records, text, costs, HP, power values, and official rules clarifications are sourced and validated directly from official publisher data:

  • Official Fantasy Flight Games API: Direct sync of all standard set releases, card attributes, and hi-res scans.
  • Complete Set Coverage: Ingesting Spark of Rebellion (SOR), Shadows of the Galaxy (SHD), Twilight of the Republic (TWI), Jump to Lightspeed (JTL), and ongoing expansions.
  • Dual-Face Mechanics: Full high-resolution front and reverse images for every Leader, showing both un-deployed setup actions and deployed unit combat stats.

Machine & Developer Interfaces

Every card page provides native machine endpoints:

  • /card/{slug}.md: Pure Markdown representation optimized for LLMs, chatbots, and AI code agents.
  • /card/{slug}.json: Structured REST JSON payload with full attributes, aspects, and arenas.
  • /llms.txt: Context discovery document for AI and search scrapers.
