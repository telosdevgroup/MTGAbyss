Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Operator: Telos Development Group LLC

1. Introduction

AvaScry Necromunda (“we”, “our”, or “us”), accessible at necromunda.avascry.com, is operated by Telos Development Group LLC. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the collection, use, and protection of information when you interact with our website and related services. We prioritize data minimization, security, and transparent privacy practices.

2. Information We Collect & Process

Server Logs & Technical Metrics: In order to maintain system uptime, prevent denial-of-service disruptions, and secure our infrastructure, our web servers automatically record routine non-identifying technical metadata. This includes your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, timestamps, and page request routes.

Contact Form Communications: When you voluntarily submit an errata report or general question via our Contact Dispatch form, we collect the name or handle you supply, your contact address (email or Discord username), the inquiry classification, and the submitted message body solely for the purpose of reviewing and responding to your message.

Client-Side Browser Storage: We may use browser local storage (localStorage) to save interface display preferences (such as filtering filters or column displays). This preference data resides solely on your client hardware and is never transmitted to our remote database servers.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We may partner with third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements on our website.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at optout.networkadvertising.org.

4. Data Protection & User Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

Under international privacy frameworks including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), visitors possess distinct legal rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request confirmation of whether we hold personal communications submitted by you.
  • Right to Rectification & Erasure: You may request the prompt deletion or correction of any contact inquiries sent to us.
  • No Sale of Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data to data brokers or unrelated commercial third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

AvaScry Necromunda is an information reference intended for general audiences and tabletop gamers. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided information through our contact form, please notify us immediately for prompt deletion.

6. Contact Information & Data Requests

For privacy inquiries, rights assertions, or questions concerning our policy practices, please contact us through our dedicated Contact Dispatch Form or email operator Telos Development Group at telosdevgroup@gmail.com.
