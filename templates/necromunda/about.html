About AvaScry Necromunda

Tactical weapon profiles, rule traits, clan house registries, and skill trees for tabletop Necromunda skirmish campaigns.

Mission & Purpose

AvaScry Necromunda is an independent, community-driven reference engine and tactical lexicon built for arbitrators, campaign leaders, and gang commanders fighting in the grim industrial depths of Necromunda's Underhive.

Our goal is to eliminate rules disputes and rulebook table-flipping during intense campaign scenarios by presenting instantly searchable, authoritative weapon statistics, weapon trait mechanics, clan house doctrines, and skill progression trees on desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.

Data Integrity & Coverage

Every armory entry and trait description is indexed from official tabletop publications, errata revisions, and rules supplements:

  • Underhive Armory Profiles: Full ranged and close-combat weapon matrices including Short/Long range brackets, accuracy modifiers (+/–), Strength, Damage, Armor Piercing (AP), Ammo rolls, and trait combinations.
  • Rules & Trait Lexicon: In-depth breakdowns of combat traits (e.g., Blast, Rapid Fire, Seismic, Versatile, Toxin, Rad-Phage) with precise mechanical resolutions.
  • Clan House Manifests: Historical background, tactical combat doctrines, house-specific equipment lists, and unique rules for Houses Escher, Goliath, Orlock, Van Saar, Delaque, and Cawdor.
  • Universal Skill Sets: Complete skill trees across Agility, Brawn, Combat, Cunning, Ferocity, Leadership, and Shooting disciplines.

Machine-Readable Architecture

In addition to our responsive human interface, AvaScry Necromunda delivers full structured Markdown (.md) and JSON (.json) representations for every weapon, trait, clan house, and skill. Machine crawlers, AI assistants, and community tabletop tools can interface via our published llms.txt and sitemap indices.
