Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

AvaScry is built with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information necessary to deliver and improve our card exploration and deck-building tools:

  • Account Information: If you choose to sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your basic profile information (name, email address, and profile picture) to maintain your saved Commander decks.
  • Usage & Analytics Data: We use aggregated, anonymized telemetry (e.g. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity) to understand page navigation, search patterns, and feature performance.
  • Local Preferences: Your theme preference (dark/light mode) and language selection are stored locally in your browser's localStorage and cookies.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and sync your drafted Commander decks across devices.
  • Improve search relevance, card synergy recommendations, and site performance.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or server abuse.

We never sell or monetize your personal data.

3. Third-Party Services

Card data and imagery are served using public Scryfall API caches and community databases. AvaScry is an independent fan project and does not share user data with card data providers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete any of your saved Commander decks directly from the Deck Manager at any time. If you wish to delete your account and all associated saved decks completely, you may request deletion by contacting support.

5. Contact Us

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data requests, please reach out via email at telosdevgroup@gmail.com.
